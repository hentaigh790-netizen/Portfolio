01. What Are Cookies & Storage Tokens?

Cookies and web storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage) are tiny text strings or state markers placed onto your device when you browse websites. They help remember preferences, ensure security during form submissions, and maintain high-fidelity interactive state without reloading the page.

This portfolio is engineered with privacy by design. We do not use aggressive ad trackers, invasive fingerprinting, or cross-site data harvesting.

03. Managing Browser Storage

You have complete control over whether cookies and storage tokens are kept on your computer:

  • Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
  • Private / Incognito Mode: Browsing in Private mode automatically discards all local storage tokens when your session ends.
  • Impact of Blocking: Disabling essential scripts may cause animation stuttering or prevent modal dialogs from remembering dismissed states.
